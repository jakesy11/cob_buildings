Sources & methods

Building footprints are physical outlines. Assessor records describe assessed buildings or groups. Their IDs are not interchangeable.

Matching and interpretation

  • Buildings intersecting the city boundary are included. Parcel geometry supports the Assessor linkage; a parcel intersecting the boundary can include buildings outside it.
  • A footprint receives a parcel association only when at least 90% of its area overlaps one parcel and no other parcel overlaps 10% or more. It remains unclassified when linked Assessor categories disagree or no record is found.
  • Building use shown on footprints is inherited from parcel-associated records. It is not a verified match to a specific assessed building.
  • Construction-era and permit filters use associated records. No footprint area is presented as gross floor area.
  • Apartment unit totals are recorded at complex/parcel scope, and are not summed across building records. Effective year is an assessment attribute, not a verified renovation date.
  • Heating-system categories do not consistently identify fuel. No missing system, energy use or emissions is estimated.
  • Commerce data is included only when supplied with an explicit Assessor inventory-ID crosswalk. Missing submissions are not evidence of noncompliance. State coverage is not inferred from gross Assessor area.
  • Condominium and small multifamily coverage requires additional source reconciliation. This is a research baseline, not a certified complete inventory.
